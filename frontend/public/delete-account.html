Account & Data Deletion Policy 📋

The developer of "Tarneeb Challenge" is fully committed to user privacy. Upon submitting this request, the following actions will be performed:

  • Data deleted permanently: Your player profile, username, registered email address, and encrypted credentials.
  • Gameplay statistics: All game history (rounds played, wins/losses, total score, and active rankings in the leaderboard).
  • Retention Period: Deletion will be fully executed and finalized within 24 to 48 hours from request submission.
  • Exceptions: Historic billing and subscription financial records are retained solely for tax and legal compliance reasons mandated by Google Play and Apple App Store, and are never used for any other purposes.
